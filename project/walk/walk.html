I climb down the stairs.

So many stairs. I count 57 steps.

Some thoughts in my head

I usually walk this route without paying much attention. Today, I decided to walk slowly and notice what I normally pass by.

My route

  1. Cross the street.
  2. Walk by the building with the mirror
  3. Turn the corner.
  4. Keep going until you see the grocery store then cross the street.
  5. Walk up the block and there you are.

Things I noticed

  • An elderly couple dining inside the French restaurant.
  • A mirror on the side of the bodega. It says "Flowers" on it.
  • Funky font from the beauty salon.

The sidewalk

The concrete looked gray at first. Looking closer, I noticed green, pink, and silver.

The corner

A bicycle passed just as I stepped off the curb. Look before crossing.

Words from a sign

FRESH FLOWERS
OPEN LATE
PLEASE RING BELL

After the walk

I covered very little distance, but found more to look at once I stopped trying to get somewhere.

Notes written from memory after returning home.
