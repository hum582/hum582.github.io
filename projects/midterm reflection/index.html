Midterm Reflection

Across my projects, I’ve noticed that I’m drawn to simple, clean layouts and fonts like Courier New that give a website a retro feel. Although my work shares a consistent minimalist style, each project has been an opportunity to explore something new, from typography and layout to animation and interaction. Looking back, I feel more confident in my ability to build a small website on my own and make design choices through code. I can use colors, backgrounds, and fonts to shape the look of a page, and add animation and interaction to make it more engaging for visitors. Next, I’d like to learn JavaScript to explore more complex interactions and experiment with creating small browser games.
